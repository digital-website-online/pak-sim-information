Official Excise Portals:

Verify complete registration, engine, chassis number, and owner details via official provincial portals:

  • Punjab Excise: mtmis.punjab.gov.pk
  • Sindh Excise: excise.gos.pk
  • Islamabad ICT: islamabadexcise.gov.pk
  • KPK Excise: kpexcise.gov.pk

Why Verify Vehicle Registration?

Verifying vehicle records before buying or selling a car or motorcycle ensures that the vehicle is legally registered, non-stolen, and free from outstanding token tax liabilities.
