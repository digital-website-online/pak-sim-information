About Pak SIM Information Portal

Finding accurate and secure public utility services online in Pakistan can be difficult due to third-party tracking sites. Pak SIM Information Portal is designed to offer a clean, safe, and direct routing portal where citizens can access official verification resources without compromising their personal data.

PECA Compliance & Safe Verification Methods

Our platform strictly adheres to the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA 2016) guidelines. We do not store, scrape, or display unauthorized personal records. All instructions guide users directly to government-sanctioned channels such as PTA SMS portals (668/667) and official provincial DLIMS servers.
