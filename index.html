Pak SIM Info & Official Verification Guides

Welcome to PakSIM Info, your primary legal guide portal for verifying official public record systems across Pakistan. Staying informed about your digital identity, registered SIM cards, and driving credentials is key to personal cybersecurity and avoiding fraud.

Why Check SIM Ownership Details?

This feature helps you stay aware, secure, and informed about the numbers registered under your CNIC identity. PTA (Pakistan Telecommunication Authority) mandates regular checking of registered numbers to ensure no unauthorized SIMs are active in your name.

  • Identifying unknown callers contacting you
  • Checking if a SIM number is genuine and registered under your identity
  • Avoiding spam, scams, financial fraud, and fake lottery schemes
  • Verifying active SIM counts for personal or work-related compliance
  • Keeping your family’s digital information secure

DLIMS – Fast & Secure Driving Licence Management System

DLIMS (Driving Licence Information Management System) is Pakistan’s official provincial platform for verifying and managing driving licence records online. Each province operates its dedicated portal, allowing citizens to check licence status, validity dates, authorized vehicle categories, and renewal deadlines instantly.

Simply utilize the step-by-step guides provided on our portal to access official police verification channels directly without visiting traffic offices in person.
