Official Electricity Portals:

Access your official bill duplicate, payment status, and history directly:

  • IESCO (Islamabad): Visit iesco.com.pk
  • LESCO (Lahore): Visit lesco.gov.pk
  • MEPCO (Multan): Visit mepco.com.pk
  • FESCO (Faisalabad): Visit fesco.com.pk
  • K-Electric (Karachi): Visit ke.com.pk

Where to find your Reference Number?

The reference number is a 14-digit numeric sequence located on the top left or right corner of your electricity bill. It uniquely identifies your meter connection across power distribution companies in Pakistan.
