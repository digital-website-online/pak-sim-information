Official Portal Steps:

You can directly access your duplicate bill via official portals:

  • Sui Northern (SNGPL): Visit www.sngpl.com.pk for Punjab, KPK & Islamabad.
  • Sui Southern (SSGC): Visit www.ssgc.com.pk for Sindh & Balochistan.

How to Find Your Account ID?

Your Consumer ID or Account Number is a unique 10 to 11 digit code printed on the top left corner of your physical monthly gas bill statement. Using this number allows you to print or view pending payment amounts and due dates instantly.
