Official Verification Channels:

You can securely track, renew, or verify CNIC identity documents via official NADRA platforms:

  • Pak-ID Portal: Visit id.nadra.gov.pk for online application tracking and renewal.
  • Pak-ID Mobile App: Download the official NADRA Pak-ID app on iOS or Android for biometric verification.
  • SMS Verification: Send your CNIC number to 8009 (from registered SIM) to verify family tree (Qaumi Tashakhuz).

Important Security Note

To protect personal identity data, never enter sensitive biometric or CNIC details on unofficial third-party websites. Always utilize NADRA’s official e-services portal or authorized SMS numbers.
