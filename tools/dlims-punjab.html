Complete Guide to DLIMS Online Driving License System

The Driving License Issuance Management System (DLIMS) phase-II automates the process of license issuance, renewal, and tracking across all districts of Punjab, Pakistan.

Key Features of DLIMS Verification

  • Learner Permit Tracking: Check current learner permit validity and scheduled test dates.
  • Regular License Status: Confirm validity for LTV, HTV, Motorcar, and Motorcycle driving permits.
  • e-License Download: Access your digital driving license via official Punjab Police applications.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How can I verify my driving license in Punjab online?

You can verify your driving license by visiting dlims.punjab.gov.pk and entering your 13-digit CNIC number under the Verification section.

How to download digital driving license in Punjab?

Download the official DLIMS Punjab app or visit the web portal to generate a downloadable PDF copy of your verified digital driving license.
